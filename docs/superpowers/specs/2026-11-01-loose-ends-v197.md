## Built

**Loading bar.** A plate in `index.html`, up from the first paint, before any module runs.
Progress is real: finished files against a total a test pins to the real import graph,
counted from resource timing. With no numbers it shows an indeterminate glint, never a
made-up percentage. It covers first load, first use of REAL 3D (three is weighted at its
byte share) and of PIXEL, and a rebuild with nothing to fetch. It only appears after about
150 ms. A failed file shows a plain message with RETRY, and a failed look also offers USE
CLASSIC 2D. Its animation stays on the compositor: with the main thread blocked 4 s, a
screencast still got 169 distinct frames.

Cost, kept on purpose: going back to REAL 3D after its 10 s release draws about 3 CLASSIC
frames (up to 82 ms, SwiftShader) while the bar paints, where before the page froze with
no feedback.

**BOOSTER marks.** Heavier brackets in all three looks (pick B of 3, from a phone-size sheet):
wider and longer, never brighter, with a gap on each side so a mark never closes into a
plate. Colours are unchanged.

**ICE brake tip.** A 17th bit `z` in `nb.coach.v2`. A player who first met ICE at CORE now
gets the brake line on first contact at a heat where the coast needs it, because V19 = 0
assumes the brake. A first meeting at MAX shows one tip, not two. 6 s stays: the longest
tip, TRAP MAX, is 16 words, 5.3 s at 3 words/s. No tip copy changed. ICE MAX and TRAP
finish during the fade, which starts at 4.5 s, so the playtest should watch those.

**REAL 3D heat look.** All 15 tile states clear the bar of 4, in normal vision and in all
three colour-vision simulations (they were 10 of 15 under it). Before and after, worst
simulated: ICE PATCH 2.11 to 4.23, PORTAL 2.48 to 4.36, BOOSTER 2.83 to 4.36, TRAP wind-up
2.62 to 4.26, open GATE 3.93 to 4.37. It lives in `three/featmesh.js` `heatLayer` and
`three/gatebars.js`, static and with no re-hue. Part of the separation is value, not
structure, and PORTAL's CORE to PLUS step rests mostly on value. The shared painters did
not move. TRAP's corner clamps are the loudest addition.

**Download size.** `src/core/board3.js`, `featplace.js` and `featchecks.js` left the boot
graph, loaded by `src/app/gen3load.js` only on `?gen=3`. Boot graph 882.2 to 807.3 KB raw,
317.1 to 293.9 KB gzip per file. This is download at boot, parse and critical path, not
first-visit bandwidth, because the service worker still precaches them for offline. Boards
are byte-identical (960 boards per generation). The tile rule modules stay in boot, about
16 KB: `step()` calls them synchronously.

**New-world freeze (#36).** First frame of every world, new or revisited, 150–167 ms to
19 ms on an M5 Max (cold profile). Programs are pinned for the renderer's life
(`three/progpin.js`), biome literals became uniforms, ambient and burst share one material,
and world textures stay per biome (2 kept), with the next room's prepared on the WIN overlay.
Under 6x CPU throttle: 51–118 ms for a new world, 117–135 ms for a revisit. **#36 stays
open:** its own bar asks for a real phone, and none was measured.

**The Descent, design only.** The design doc, a 16-foe roster and a layer 1 slice plan are on
local branch `descent-design`, unmerged and never to be pushed by an agent, because the
doc holds the whole story. The roster sheet is git-ignored. No game code was written.
